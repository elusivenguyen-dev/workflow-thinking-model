## Core Principles

1. **Multiplicity over Singularity**: Hold 3-5 plausible futures simultaneously, never one
2. **Plausibility over Probability**: Explore what *could* happen, not just what is *likely*
3. **Actionability over Prediction**: Scenarios exist to inform present decisions, not predict future states
4. **Divergence before Convergence**: Explore widely before narrowing to strategic implications
5. **Temporal Layering**: Distinguish near-term (1-2 years), medium-term (3-7 years), long-term (10+ years)
6. **Uncertainty Mapping**: Explicitly identify and quantify known unknowns and unknown unknowns
7. **Stakeholder Perspective Integration**: Each scenario must account for multiple actor viewpoints
8. **Feedback Loops**: Scenarios must be updated as reality unfolds and assumptions shift

## Scenario Types Taxonomy

| Type | Description | Time Horizon | Primary Use |
|------|-------------|--------------|-------------|
| **Exploratory** | "What might happen?" | Long-term | Broad environmental scanning |
| **Normative** | "What should happen?" | Medium-term | Vision and goal-setting |
| **Strategic** | "What if X happens?" | Medium-term | Strategy stress-testing |
| **Tactical** | "What if competitor does Y?" | Short-term | Operational contingency |
| **Wildcard** | "What if the impossible happens?" | Variable | Resilience and preparedness |
| **Counterfactual** | "What if history had been different?" | Retrospective | Learning and pattern recognition |
| **Convergence** | "What if multiple trends collide?" | Medium-term | Complex systems analysis |
| **Divergence** | "What if a single variable splits the future?" | Variable | Decision tree construction |

## Key Diagnostic Questions

### For Individuals (L1-L3)
- Can I comfortably hold contradictory futures in my mind without anxiety?
- Do I regularly question my own assumptions about "the way things will be"?
- Can I identify at least three different ways my current project/situation could evolve?

### For Teams (L4-L5)
- Does our planning process explicitly include "what if we're wrong" phases?
- Do we have documented scenarios that are updated quarterly?
- Can team members articulate the key uncertainties driving our strategy?

### For Organizations (L6-L7)
- Does the board review scenarios at least annually?
- Is scenario thinking embedded in our capital allocation process?
- Do we have pre-agreed trigger points for activating contingency plans?

### For Ecosystems (L8-L12)
- Do we participate in industry-wide scenario exercises?
- Are our AI systems trained on multi-future datasets?
- Do we contribute to global futures research and public policy foresight?
